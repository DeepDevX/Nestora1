NESTORA - e-commerce website prototype (EBU4 Project Part 2)
Imaginary company: Nestora, www.nestora.shop

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click index.html (works offline in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

PAGES
  index.html     Home
  products.html  Products (filters, sorting, "will it fit?" checker)
  product.html   Product detail (buy or rent, quantity, add to cart)
  cart.html      Shopping cart (quantities, promo code NESTORA10, demo checkout)
  about.html     About Us
  contact.html   Contact (form validation, FAQ)

NOTES
  - Built by hand with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No paid services were used.
  - The cart is saved in the browser (localStorage). No payments are taken.
  - Product pictures are original SVG illustrations drawn in code.
  - The contact form checks entries and shows a confirmation; it does not send email.
