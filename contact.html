Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How does renting work?

Choose Rent on a product page and pay monthly. After three months you can cancel, swap to another item, or buy it at a reduced price.

Will it fit through my door?

Every product lists its dimensions. The sofa bed is built in sections and fits an 80 cm doorway. Use the fit checker on the shop page for floor space.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 3 to 5 business days. Orders over $99 ship free, and assembly is available for an extra fee.

What is your returns policy?

You have 30 days to return an item. Returned items are refurbished and resold so they do not go to waste.
