About Nestora

Small spaces, smarter living.

Our story

We started with a sofa that would not fit

Nestora began when our founder, Maya, tried to carry a new sofa up the stairs of her first apartment. It did not fit. It went back to the store, and the cost of the return was more than the sofa was worth.

She realised that people renting small places have a different problem from homeowners. Their rooms are tight, they move often, and they do not want to be stuck with furniture that does not travel well.

So we built a store where the room comes first. Tell us your space, we show what fits, and you choose whether to buy it or rent it.

Our mission

To make furnishing a small rental simple, affordable and easy to undo.

Who we serve

  • Students and first-time renters
  • Young professionals in small condos
  • Newcomers setting up a first home
  • Downsizers moving to smaller places
